KAGE renderer (based on HowardZorn/kage-engine)
================================================

Source: https://github.com/HowardZorn/kage-engine (dev branch), GPL-3.0.
Integrated under Scripts/kage/renderer/ and imported via Scripts/kage/engine.py.

Local patches (keep when syncing upstream):
- stroke.py get_box: use real stroke type (not opt→6), skip non-finite coords
- kage.py: pad stroke columns with 0 instead of NaN
- components.py search: always return "" on miss (ignore_version fallthrough)
- serif_stroke_drawer.py draw_line: distinct Vec2 list; match a1/a2 with % 100

To update from upstream, copy into this package and re-apply the patches above.
